# ADR-005 — Editorial Evidence-Led Portfolio Presentation

- **Status:** Accepted (implemented, refinement in progress)
- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Context:** The visual direction (recorded in `docs/portfolio-brand-system.md`, `docs/phase-3b-visual-direction-report.md`, `docs/phase-3c-visual-refinement-report.md` and the `product-portfolio-taste` skill) moved the portfolio from card-heavy SaaS presentation toward an editorial, evidence-led system. Public pages use a shared 1200px shell, role-based type scale, and evidence-first project presentation. The redesign is in progress on `agent/portfolio-redesign`.
- **Decision:** The portfolio prioritizes project evidence, typography, whitespace, and editorial composition over card-heavy SaaS presentation.
- **Rationale:** The owner is a product designer; the portfolio's job is to communicate product judgment, and evidence-led editorial treatment supports that.
- **Alternatives:** Card-grid SaaS presentation (rejected as the primary system); decorative/agency treatment (rejected by the taste skill).
- **Consequences:** Headlines express decisions/outcomes rather than labels; images are treated as evidence; motion stays restrained; pages use shared tokens rather than per-page conventions.
- **Constraints:** Approved direction may only change through explicit owner approval; the taste skill must be consulted for UI decisions.
