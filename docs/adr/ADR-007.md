# ADR-007 — Services Is Not a Primary Product Surface

- **Status:** Accepted (implemented)
- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Context:** `app/services/page.tsx` performs a permanent redirect to `/about#capabilities`. Capabilities are presented through the about page and the homepage "Capabilities" section fed by `Skill` records. Service records (`Service` model) exist for backward compatibility.
- **Decision:** Services is compatibility-only and redirected according to current implementation. It is not a primary portfolio surface.
- **Rationale:** The portfolio positions around product judgment and capabilities, not a services menu; a redirect preserves old links.
- **Alternatives:** Full services page (rejected: dilutes the evidence-led positioning); deleting the route (rejected: breaks links without benefit).
- **Consequences:** Future work should treat `/services` as a redirect target; do not re-expand it into a primary surface without owner approval.
- **Constraints:** The redirect must be permanent; no content duplication with about/capabilities.
